❌ Myths vs ✅ Facts

Myth

Donating blood makes you permanently weak.


Fact:

A healthy eligible donor can normally recover after donation.

Myth

Blood donation is only useful during accidents.


Fact:

Blood is also required for surgeries, childbirth emergencies and blood disorders.

Myth

All blood groups can receive blood from anyone.


Fact:

Blood groups have compatibility rules. Matching is important before transfusion.

Myth

Having recovered from malaria means you can never donate.


Fact:

Indian blood-centre guidance includes a deferral period after full recovery rather than treating every past malaria case as a permanent ban.
